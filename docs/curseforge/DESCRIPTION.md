# Tricky Trials Backport

All of 1.21 Tricky Trials and the drops after it, through 1.21.11, on Minecraft 1.12.2. It needs Backport Core on the client and the server.

> 💬 [Join the Discord](https://discord.gg/kxQvMDJBTN) for bug reports and questions.

## Trial chambers

Trial chambers generate underground, built from 1.21's own structure files, where 1.21 puts them for the same seed. Trial spawners wake when a player comes near, send a wave sized to the number of players, and pay out loot once it's beaten. Trial keys open vaults; each vault opens once per player.

Drink an ominous bottle near a trial spawner and Bad Omen turns into Trial Omen. Spawners go ominous: stronger, armed mobs, items falling from above, and ominous keys for ominous vaults, which hold the heavy core.

## The breeze and the bogged

The breeze guards the chambers. It jumps around you, shoots wind charges and bats your arrows back. It drops breeze rods, which make wind charges you can throw yourself: they knock things back, open doors and trapdoors, flip levers and launch you into the air.

The bogged is a mossy skeleton of the swamps and chambers. It shoots poison arrows, and shears take its mushrooms.

## The mace

A heavy core and a breeze rod make the mace. Hit something after a fall and the damage grows with the drop, your fall is canceled and nearby mobs are knocked away. Three enchantments of its own: Density, Breach and Wind Burst.

## The crafter

The crafter crafts one item per redstone pulse and spits it out the front. Click a slot to lock it.

![A crafter in a trial chamber, between candles on copper grates](TODO-Banner-14.jpg)

## Copper and tuff

*   **Copper bulbs**: switch on and off with each redstone pulse, and dim as they oxidize.
*   **Copper doors, trapdoors and grates**, and chiseled copper. All of them oxidize, scrape and wax.
*   **Tuff**: stairs, slabs and walls, polished tuff, tuff bricks and chiseled forms.

## The copper golem

Put a carved pumpkin on a block of copper to build one. It carries items from copper chests to the chests around it. Left to oxidize all the way, it freezes into a statue.

Copper also makes tools, armor, horse armor, torches, lanterns, bars and chains.

![Copper armor, a copper golem statue, shelves and a copper chest](TODO-Banner-10.jpg)

## The pale garden

A gray forest inside the dark forest. Pale oak comes in every wood shape. Pale moss hangs from the trees, and eyeblossoms open at night.

The creaking walks there after dark. It freezes while you watch it and can't be killed while its creaking heart lives. Hitting it grows resin on the heart. Resin makes resin bricks in every stone shape.

## Spring

Leaf litter and wildflowers carpet forests, fireflies drift around firefly bushes at night, and fallen trees, bushes, dry grass and cactus flowers turn up where 1.21.5 puts them.

Cows, pigs and chickens come in warm and cold variants by biome. Blue and brown eggs hatch the matching chicken. Wolves get their sound variants.

## The happy ghast

A dried ghast left in water hatches a ghastling. It grows into a happy ghast that carries up to four players. Put a harness on it to fly.

## Spears and the nautilus

Spears in seven materials: jab for quick hits, hold to charge. Lunge pushes you forward. Mobs carry them too.

Tame a nautilus with pufferfish and ride it underwater; its rider breathes. Zombie nautiluses roam the oceans. Nautilus armor comes in five tiers.

The parched, a desert skeleton, shoots arrows of Weakness. Zombie horses spawn in plains and savannas, sometimes ridden. Horses no longer sink while you ride them.

## Items and blocks

*   **Bundles**, in sixteen colors, hold a stack's worth of mixed items.
*   **Shelves** in every wood show three items and swap them with your hotbar.
*   **Effects**: Weaving, Oozing, Infested and Wind Charged, with their potions and arrows.
*   **Paintings**: the twenty-one of 1.21 and 1.21.7.
*   **Banner patterns**: Flow, Guster, Field Masoned and Bordure Indented.
*   **Music discs**: Precipice, Creator, Creator (Music Box), Tears and Lava Chicken.
*   **Pottery sherds and trim templates**: flow, guster, scrape and bolt.

## The rest of 1.21

You also get the locator bar, which shows where other players are, 1.21's smaller changes to things 1.12.2 already has, and 1.21.11's title screen, menu music and splashes.

Every feature can be turned off in Backport Core's settings screen, under Mod Options.

## Compatibility

**[Caves & Cliffs Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/caves-cliffs-backport)**: copper and tuff are its own. In its 1.18 worlds, trial chambers start at 1.21's depth. Without it, this mod brings its own copper and tuff.

**[Village & Pillage Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/villager-backport)**: Trial Omen comes from its Bad Omen and ominous bottle. Its loom takes the new banner patterns, its stonecutter cuts tuff, and its composter takes the new plants. Without it, this mod has its own Bad Omen and ominous bottle.

**[Update Aquatic Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/update-aquatic-backport)**: its pufferfish tame the nautilus, and its drowned ride zombie nautiluses.

**[Buzzy Bees Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/buzzy-bees-backport)**: honeycomb waxes copper.

**[Nether Update Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/nether-update-backport)**: netherite spears, nautilus armor and horse armor, and crimson and warped shelves.

**[Trails & Tales Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/trails-tales-backport)**: the new sherds go on decorated pots, the new templates and resin trim armor, and pale oak, cherry and bamboo get hanging signs and shelves.

**[Wild Update Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/wild-update-backport)**: the bogged spawn in its mangrove swamps, and pale oak gets a chest boat and mangrove a shelf.

**OptiFine**: works, shaders included.

**Fluidlogged API** (optional): copper grates and trapdoors, copper chests and statues, tuff, resin and copper stairs and slabs, shelves, the heavy core and the dried ghast hold water. Without it they stay dry and everything else works.

## Requirements

Minecraft 1.12.2 with Forge 14.23.5.2847 or newer, and [Backport Core](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/backport-core) 1.4.2 or newer. The CurseForge app installs Backport Core with it. [Fluidlogged API](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/fluidlogged-api) 3.3.3 or newer is optional, for waterlogged blocks.
