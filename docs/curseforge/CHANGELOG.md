# 1.0.3

*   **Dynamic pale oaks** with Dynamic Trees - Backport installed: pale gardens grow its dynamic pale oaks, and pale oak saplings grow into them.
*   **Falling leaves**: oak, birch, jungle, acacia and dark oak leaves, and other mods' leaves, Dynamic Trees' too, now and then drop a leaf in their own color, as in 1.21.5. Spruce drops none; pale oak, cherry and poplar keep their own. Switch it off with Spring Plants > Falling Leaves.

Needs Backport Core 1.4.9 or newer.

# 1.0.2

*   **1.21's look and feel is now Backport Core's**: Reduce FPS when, High Contrast Block Outlines, Invert Mouse X, Sprint Window and Save Unsent Chats, the air bubbles and their pop, chat drafts, item rarity colors, the rain and nether fog, the sunrise in rain, the loading screen, the F3 entity line, the slot highlight, music toast and ghast textures, the splashes, the startup logo and 1.21.11's wording. Needs Backport Core 1.4.7 or newer.

# 1.0.1

*   **Realistic Terrain Generation** (and RTG Unofficial): pale gardens now generate in RTG worlds, on RTG's dark forest terrain. RTG turned them into plains. Needs Backport Core 1.4.5.
*   **Fixed: trial chambers showing at the surface.** A chamber now sits low enough to keep at least six blocks of ground over every part of it, and one too tall to fit under the ground or sea bed where it starts is not built there, so none break through into fields, oceans or Realistic Terrain Generation's low land. `/locate` and trial explorer maps only find chambers that are built. Worlds with Caves & Cliffs Backport's extended height keep 1.21's depths unchanged.
*   **The pale oak boat with chest**, with Wild Update Backport: a pale oak boat and a chest, as in 1.21.4.
*   **Boats are now Backport Core's**, one boat and one boat with chest for every backport's wood (needs Backport Core 1.4.4). Pale oak boats look, float, steer and drop as before, and boats already in your worlds are kept, with whoever rides them.
*   **Every painting in the creative inventory**: the Decoration Blocks tab and JEI list the random painting, then each painting on its own, 1.12.2's 26 and the 21 new ones, with title, artist and size in the tooltip. Such a painting hangs exactly that picture where it fits, and middle-clicking a hung painting picks that one. Paintings in survival stay random.
*   **Poplar shelves** with Game Drops Backport, made from six stripped poplar logs like every other shelf.
*   **Pale oaks at Game Drops Backport's abandoned camps** are full worldgen pale oaks, with their pale moss.
*   **Leaf litter in Game Drops Backport's dappled forest** takes 26.3's orange-brown dry foliage color.
*   **Named paintings**: a painting renamed in an anvil hangs with its name. With Game Drops Backport, as in 26.3, the name shows when you look at the painting, and the painting drops back with its name when broken.
*   **Fixed: a painting saved with a `minecraft:` variant id came back as Baroque.**
*   Trial chambers are laid out by Backport Core's shared modern jigsaw placer, block for block as before.

*   **The ominous bottle is now this mod's**, since 1.21 added it, also with Village & Pillage Backport installed. Drinking one gives Bad Omen at the bottle's level, I to V, for 100 minutes. With Village & Pillage 2.7.4, pillager captains drop it instead of giving Bad Omen, and its Bad Omen turns into Raid Omen in a village, as in 1.21.
*   **Ominous bottles from older Village & Pillage worlds become this mod's bottles**, with the same level, when Village & Pillage 2.7.4 is installed.
*   **Fixed: a spawn egg used on a cow, pig or chicken made a temperate baby.** The baby now has its parent's variant, as in 1.21.5.

Needs Backport Core 1.4.4 or newer.

# 1.0.0

First release: all of 1.21 Tricky Trials and the drops after it, through 1.21.11, on Minecraft 1.12.2.

*   **Trial chambers**, with trial spawners, vaults, trial keys, Trial Omen and ominous trials.
*   **The breeze and wind charges**, and **the bogged**.
*   **The mace**, with Density, Breach and Wind Burst.
*   **The crafter**.
*   **Copper and tuff**: copper bulbs, doors, trapdoors, grates and chiseled copper; tuff stairs, slabs, walls, polished tuff and tuff bricks; copper tools, armor, torches, lanterns, bars and chains.
*   **The copper golem**, copper chests and copper statues.
*   **The pale garden**: pale oak, pale moss, eyeblossoms, the creaking and its heart, and resin.
*   **Spring**: leaf litter, wildflowers, firefly bushes, fallen trees, bushes, dry grass, cactus flowers, and warm and cold cows, pigs and chickens.
*   **The happy ghast**, the ghastling and the dried ghast.
*   **Spears and the nautilus**, zombie nautiluses and nautilus armor.
*   **The parched and zombie horses.**
*   **Bundles, shelves, the trial explorer map**, the new effects, potions, paintings, banner patterns, music discs, pottery sherds and trim templates.
*   **The locator bar**, 1.21's smaller changes to things 1.12.2 already has, and 1.21.11's title screen, menu music and splashes.
*   **Not in Minecraft**: open eyeblossoms give off a dim light at night. Turn off **Glowing Eyeblossoms** on the Pale Garden settings page for vanilla's unlit flowers.
*   Every feature has its own setting in Backport Core's settings screen.
*   Translated into 18 languages.
*   Requires Backport Core 1.4.2 or newer.
