# Tricky Trials Backport: photos

The tester's 22 images in `E:\MincraftModding\trickytrialphotos`. The Banners are 2712 x 1220 phone captures (20:9) with
shaders on, sharp, about 1 MB each. Every Banner has the launcher's **"..." button in the top-left corner**: crop it out
before uploading (a 16:9 crop of 2168 x 1220 starting at x 200 or more clears it).

In `DESCRIPTION.md`, each image line points at `TODO-Banner-N.jpg`, which stands for `Banner N.jpg`. Replace it with
the image's CurseForge URL once uploaded.

## Featured image

**Not `Screenshot_2026-11-19-20-50-20-676_dev.horizon.android.dev4.jpg`.** Its 1892 x 1220 pixels are enough, but it
is saved at 57 KB, about 0.2 bits per pixel: the item icons and grate patterns are smeared with JPEG blocking at full
size. It is also nearly black, and it is a test lineup of blocks floating in a void, not a scene. Ask the tester for
the original PNG if it's wanted; as it is, it will look muddy.

**Use `Banner 15.jpg` instead**: a trial spawner centered in a small chamber room, lit by three copper bulbs, tuff bricks
and copper all around. Sharp, symmetrical, and readable at thumbnail size. Runner-up: `Banner 12.jpg` (bright copper
interior).
